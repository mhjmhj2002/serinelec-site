Technical Training

Specialised courses in electrical knowledge transfer, accident-risk prevention, tariffs and energy efficiency.

Practical methods for technical knowledge transfer

Serinelec designs training so participants can understand and confidently apply core electrical concepts. Programmes range from technological education for teaching staff to technical updating and safety in industrial plants and mining operations.

Programme objective

To apply rigorous electrical concepts through practical and educational methods for immediate use at work or in the classroom.

Main topics

Electricity in study programmes: fundamentals and practical applications.
Energy, effects and generation: renewable sources and Chile's generation matrix.
Electrical circuits: components, diagrams and applicable technical standards.
Electrical variables: field measurement, instruments and voltage, current and power readings.
Electrical supply: utility structures and informed consumer rights and duties.
Electrical risk prevention: causes, physiological effects and preventive measures.
Electrical tariffs and energy savings: bills, contracted power and efficiency.
