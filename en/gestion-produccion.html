Industrial Management and Production

Technical and administrative consulting to optimise electrical-equipment manufacturing processes.

Control and efficiency for electrical industry

Serinelec advises power-transformer and metering-equipment factories on management and production. The service combines technical expertise and administrative tools to support industrial operations.

Scope

Production planning and control: processes, resources and delivery dates.
Power transformers: support for manufacturing units up to 4 MVA and 35 kV.
Metering equipment: administration and control of associated processes.
Operational efficiency: tools for coordination and decision-making.

Applied perspective

The service is tailored to each factory, combining production follow-up, technical criteria and appropriate industrial-management practices.
