Technical Works Inspection (ITO)

Technical verification of electrical works to monitor quality, safety and regulatory compliance.

Technical control at every stage of the work

We perform technical inspection of electrical works, supporting installation review and objective documentation of field conditions. ITO is Chile's technical-works inspection practice and is applied to each project's scope.

Inspection activities

Review of electrical panels, protective devices and circuit identification.
Verification of chambers, conduits, conductors and raceways.
Comparison with applicable technical requirements, including NCh 4/2003 where relevant.
Simple or detailed reports with photographic records.
Technical follow-up of corrective actions and critical installation points.

Information for safer decisions

Inspection results organise field evidence for decision-making, work follow-up and treatment of identified non-conformities.
